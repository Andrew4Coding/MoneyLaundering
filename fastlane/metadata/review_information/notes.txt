The app works fully without an account (local-only mode) — reviewers can skip sign-in.
Optional "Sign in with Apple" only enables private iCloud sync across devices; use any Apple ID.

Feature notes:
- Bill photo parsing and smart category suggestions use on-device Apple Intelligence and
  require an Apple Intelligence-capable device + iOS with Apple Intelligence enabled. On
  unsupported devices these features degrade gracefully (manual entry / heuristic fallback).
- Siri: "Add a transaction in Swiftlet", "Show today's transactions in Swiftlet".
- Account deletion: Account tab > Delete Account (removes all local + iCloud data and signs out).

No third-party SDKs, ads, analytics, or tracking. All data stays in the user's private CloudKit database.
