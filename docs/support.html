Swiftlet Support

Swiftlet is a private, offline expense tracker for everyday spending in Indonesian rupiah. If you hit a problem or have a request, email andrewdevitoaryo@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app works fully offline. Sign in with Apple is optional and only turns on sync across your devices through your own private iCloud.
Where is my data?
On your device, and in your private iCloud if you sign in. See the privacy policy.
Natural-language or Siri entry isn't working.
On-device parsing needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence. On other devices, enter transactions manually.
How do I import my old transactions?
Use the JSON import option in the app and select a file in the expected format.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual items in the app, then sync. Deleting the app removes its local data.
